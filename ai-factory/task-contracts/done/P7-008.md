## Objective

with its production facade in `agent/cmd/agentd/main.go` and add it to the registry; add its request decoder
to `DefaultRequestDecoders()` in `agent/transport/server.go`. After this, all TWELVE capabilities are
discoverable via `GET /operations`, applicable via `POST /apply`, readable via `GET /read/{capability}`, and
included in `GET /state`.
