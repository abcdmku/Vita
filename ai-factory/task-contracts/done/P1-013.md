## Objective
Make the three merged transactional capabilities **runtime-discoverable + applicable** by registering
them in `agentd` (each capability's contract deferred its own main.go wiring to avoid concurrent edits).
Wire `nodeconfig` (P1-007), `nodetime` (P1-009), and `hostname` (P1-010) into the registry the transport
serves, so `GET /capabilities` lists all three and `POST /apply` can route a plan to any of them.
- Construct each capability with its production facade (sysdeps-backed for time/hostname, the real state
  path for nodeconfig) in `main.go`; register them in the `capabilities.Registry` the transport uses.
- Keep the loopback-only bind and the fail-closed /apply boundary (P1-008) unchanged.

## User value
This is the moment the agent actually exposes its capabilities to the controller (FR-004 discover,
FR-007 apply) — the registry was empty until now.

## Non-goals
- No new capabilities, no transport changes beyond registration, no new syscalls. Wiring + a test only.

## Dependencies
P1-007 (nodeconfig), P1-008 (transport + registry-serving), P1-009 (nodetime), P1-010 (hostname). All merged.

## Acceptance tests
