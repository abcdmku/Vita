That violates the R3 transaction rule: error paths must leave the live system unchanged. Preserve `cloneSnapshot(prior)` for undo after validating/parsing it for cursor checks.

- `agent/capabilities/pdssync/pdssync.go:656-662`: CID base32 decoding uses Go’s standard `base32` decoder without checking non-zero unused trailing bits. That accepts non-canonical malformed CIDv1 strings that P5-003 rejects. This violates the contract’s malformed-CID fail-closed requirement. Mirror the SDK’s explicit base32 decoder/trailing-bit check.
